## 修复（Fixes）

- 网页端上传或粘贴的图片现在能在客户端里显示，不再只显示文件名。
  Pictures uploaded or pasted in the web app now show in the client instead of only their file name.
- 点头像跳到私聊后，鼠标光标不再一直停在小手上。
  After clicking an avatar to open a direct message, the pointer no longer stays a pointing hand.

## 新功能（New features）

- 聊天界面右上角去掉了搜索、会话详情和「⋯」三个图标；搜索仍可用 ⌘F，频道改名在侧边栏。
  The search, details and "⋯" icons at the top right of a conversation are gone; search is still on ⌘F and channels are renamed from the sidebar.
- 头像弹窗里换头像和改名字的入口改成名字右边的小铅笔图标。
  In the avatar popover, editing your photo and name is now a small pencil beside your name.
