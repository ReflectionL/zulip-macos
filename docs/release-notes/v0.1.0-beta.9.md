## 修复（Fixes）

- 修复发送消息后输入框内容没有清空的问题。
  Fixed the composer keeping the text of a message after it was sent.
- 修复在打开的对话中「标为未读」立即失效的问题：标为未读的消息现在保持未读，直到切换到其他对话。
  Fixed "Mark as Unread" being undone at once in an open conversation: the message now stays unread until you open another conversation.
- 修复个人状态在接近 60 字上限且包含组合 emoji 时无法保存的问题。
  Fixed a status near the 60-character limit failing to save when it contained a multi-part emoji.
- 底部输入框变矮（单行 42 pt），附件和发送按钮略微缩小。
  The composer is shorter (42 pt for one line), with slightly smaller attach and send buttons.

## 新功能（New features）

- 本版无新功能。
  No new features in this release.
