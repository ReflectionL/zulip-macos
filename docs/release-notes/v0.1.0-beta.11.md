## 修复（Fixes）

- 修复悬停显示的消息时间分钟只有一位的问题：12:05 不再显示成 12:5。
  Fixed message times shown on hover dropping the leading zero of the minutes: 12:05 no longer shows as 12:5.
- 修复窗口停在底部时新消息偶尔不自动滚动到底的问题。
  Fixed the timeline sometimes not scrolling to a new message while the window was already at the bottom.
- 消息流上下边缘的渐隐更长、更柔和，不再显得突兀。
  The fades at the top and bottom of the timeline are longer and softer, so they no longer look abrupt.

## 新功能（New features）

- 界面质感改版：统一的靛蓝强调色，柔和的发送者名字颜色和频道色点，更清晰的字号层级，统一的圆角、描边、阴影和悬停/选中状态，以及更一致的动画（遵循「减弱动态效果」设置）。
  A visual refresh: one indigo accent, muted sender-name and channel colors, a clearer type scale, consistent corner radii, strokes, shadows and hover and selected states, and more consistent motion that follows Reduce Motion.
- 消息块的发送时间显示在名字旁边；侧边栏频道的「+」和展开箭头只在悬停或选中时显示，右键菜单新增「New topic…」。
  A message block shows its time next to the sender's name; a channel's "+" and disclosure arrow appear only on hover or selection, and its context menu adds "New topic…".

Whether the indigo accent, name colors, shadows and line spacing look right in light and dark appearance, whether the hover-only "+" works for you, and whether the fades and auto-scroll behave.
