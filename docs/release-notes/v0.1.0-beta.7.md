## 修复（Fixes）

- 锁屏、显示器休眠或切换用户期间收到的消息不再被标为已读，Dock 红点和通知恢复正常；解锁后刷新当前会话。
  Messages received while the screen is locked, the display is asleep or another user is active are no longer marked read; the Dock badge and notifications now appear, and the open conversation refreshes on unlock.
- 输入框改为单行：附件按钮、输入区和发送按钮位于同一行。
  The composer is now a single row, with the attach button, text field and send button side by side.
- 附件按钮的点击区域扩大为圆形，并带有悬停高亮。
  The attach button has a larger circular hit area with a hover highlight.
- 日期分隔线（Today / Yesterday）在跨过午夜、时区变化、切回应用或唤醒后会重新计算。
  Date separators (Today / Yesterday) now update after midnight, a time zone change, app activation and wake from sleep.
- 12 小时制下，悬停显示的消息时间带上午/下午标记。
  Hover timestamps show AM/PM on 12-hour clocks.
- 消息悬停操作栏移至消息右上角上方，不再遮挡已读头像。
  The message hover toolbar now sits above the message's top-right corner and no longer covers read receipts.

## 新功能（New features）

- 自定义状态：点击左下角头像，可设置表情和文字状态，或选用 Zulip 预设状态，与网页版同步。
  Custom status: click your avatar at the bottom left to set an emoji and text, or pick one of Zulip's presets; it syncs with the web app.
- 状态显示：他人的状态表情显示在消息发送人和私信列表的名字后，私信顶栏显示状态文字。
  Status display: other people's status emoji appear after their names in messages and the direct-message list, and the status text appears in the direct-message header.
- 隐身模式：在头像面板中开启后，其他人将看到你处于离线状态。
  Invisible mode: turn it on from the avatar panel to appear offline to others.
- 消息来源：在 Mentions、Starred 和频道视图中，悬停消息时在发送人后显示「#频道 › 话题」。
  Message source: in Mentions, Starred and channel views, hovering a message shows "#channel › topic" after the sender's name.

**待确认 / Feedback needed**：锁屏期间的红点与通知、单行输入框、跨午夜的日期标签、状态与隐身模式在网页版的同步，以及悬停操作栏和话题标签的位置。
Dock badge and notifications while locked, the single-row composer, date labels after midnight, status and invisible-mode sync with the web app, and the placement of the hover toolbar and topic label.
