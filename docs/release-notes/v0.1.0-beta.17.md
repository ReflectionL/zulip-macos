## 新功能（New features）

- 可以在应用里换头像和改名字：点左下角自己的头像，选“Edit photo and name…”（齿轮菜单里也有）。照片会裁成正方形后上传，保存后侧边栏和聊天记录里马上更新。
  Change your photo and name in the app: click your avatar at the bottom left and choose "Edit photo and name…" (also in the gear menu). The photo is cropped to a square before upload, and the sidebar and timeline update as soon as it is saved.
- 在频道或话题里点别人的头像，直接打开和 TA 的私聊。
  Click someone's avatar in a channel or topic to open your direct messages with them.
- 别人改了名字或头像后，打开着的聊天记录也会跟着更新。
  When someone changes their name or photo, the open conversation follows.
