## 修复（Fixes）

- 修复不在底部时发送消息后，视图停在离最新消息差一点的位置；打开会话时的位置也一并修正。
  Fixed the timeline stopping just short of the newest message after sending from higher up, and when opening a conversation.
- 侧边栏的在线状态小灯更醒目：在线与空闲使用更鲜明的颜色，离线显示为空心圆圈。
  Presence dots in the sidebar are easier to read: brighter colors for active and idle, and a hollow ring for offline.
- 设置状态后立即显示，不再等待服务器回推；保存失败时窗口顶部会显示原因。
  A saved status shows at once instead of waiting for the server to report it back, and a failed save now shows its reason at the top of the window.

## 新功能（New features）

- 主窗口顶部会显示操作提示（例如保存失败、频道已重命名），6 秒后自动消失。
  The main window shows notices at the top (such as a failed save or a renamed channel), which fade after six seconds.
